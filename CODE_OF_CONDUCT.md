# Code of conduct

Teremoq contributors are expected to collaborate professionally, respectfully
and in good faith.

Acceptable participation includes constructive technical disagreement,
evidence-based review, respect for privacy and attribution, and patience with
different backgrounds and levels of experience. Harassment, threats,
discrimination, unwanted sexual attention, doxxing, deliberate exposure of
secrets, and sustained disruption are not acceptable.

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions that violate these expectations
and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation. Enforcement should
be proportionate, documented privately where appropriate, and avoid publishing
sensitive personal information.

Security reports follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) and must use GitHub Private
Vulnerability Reporting, never a public issue. For conduct occurring on GitHub,
participants may also use GitHub's private content-reporting and abuse-reporting
features.
